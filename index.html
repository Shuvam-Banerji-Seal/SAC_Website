A Campus In Print: The Work And Life Of Its Societies

The Student Activity Council brings together the cultural, academic, sports, and residential societies at IISER Kolkata under a single administrative body.

From the Editorial Desk · SAC Communications

The Student Activity Council oversees the year-round calendar of campus life — coordinating societies, sports clubs, academic committees, residential bodies, and the institute's contingent for the Inter-IISER Cultural Meet. This is a broad ecosystem of performance, debate, inquiry, and expression.

Every body below is represented on a card bearing its logo, name, and a short introduction drawn from its official records. Read a card's full profile to browse office bearers, images, events, and achievements.

New clubs and committees will be added as they are constituted. This is a living document — printed in code, updated as each academic year unfolds.
