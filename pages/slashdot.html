Slashdot — Coding & Design Club

Slashdot is the Coding and Design Club of IISER Kolkata: a community where people learn coding and graphic design while having fun. Coding, design and AI skills are essential today and beneficial for future endeavours, and the club welcomes everyone who is interested — no prior experience necessary.
