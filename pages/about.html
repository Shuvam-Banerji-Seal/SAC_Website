About the SAC

The Student Activity Council (SAC) is the elected student body that coordinates activity at IISER Kolkata. We work with 33 clubs and committees across five bodies — Academics, Cultural, Sports, Hostel, and Food & Hygiene — to plan events, allocate resources, and represent the student community.

What we do

  • Coordinate the academic-year event calendar across clubs.
  • Manage budgets and shared resources for cultural activities.
  • Select and send delegations to the Inter-IISER Cultural Meet (IICM).
  • Run the annual election for office bearers across all clubs.

How the Council works

Office bearers are elected each academic year across all 33 bodies. The General Secretaries convene the Council; each body runs its own calendar, budget, and event approvals through the semester. Records of every tenure — portraits, documents, and event photographs — are filed in this archive, so the next council inherits a printable history rather than a blank page.

Questions, ideas, or feedback? Reach out via your club's office bearers, or look out for SAC office hours during the semester.
