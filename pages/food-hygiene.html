SAC Food & Hygiene (SMC)

The SAC Food & Hygiene committee runs the Students' Monitored Canteen (SMC) sub-committee — the student body that keeps campus dining accountable. Working alongside the canteen contractor and the institute's medical unit, the committee reviews menus and prices, runs regular quality checks and market surveys, and operates dedicated grievance channels for both students and canteen workers.

The 2025–26 sub-committee is organised into working cells: Menu & Price Review, Quality Check, Market Survey, Workers' Grievance Cell, Communication & Students' Grievance Cell, Repairs & Maintenance, Administration & Finance, Cyber Cell, Institute Orders, and Misconduct Handling. Complaints about food quality, hygiene, or pricing reach the right cell through the shared grievance form and the committee's email.
