SYDC — Self Defence Club

The Self Defence and Yoga Club (SYDC) of IISER Kolkata consists of two components: self-defense and yoga. The self-defense wing forges students into competent combatants through training in grappling-heavy MMA, boxing, wrestling, taekwondo, BJJ, and judo, with regular sparring sessions twice a week for grappling and once a week for striking sports (technical sparring only).

The Yoga wing works for overall holistic development of the body, making members capable of handling academic and personal challenges through regular yoga and meditation practice, with active participation from BS-MS students, PhD students, and faculty members.

Events & Activities

Self-defense events include Freshers Grappling Event, Calisthenics Competition, Strongman Competition (in coalition with the Gym Club), Inter-Batch Grappling Event, and Freestyle/Greco-Roman Wrestling Event. The Yoga wing holds regular meditation and yoga sessions and participates in International Yoga Day.

Achievements

The club focuses on building practical unarmed combat skills and holistic wellness through its dual-wing structure, creating a community where students develop both physical and mental resilience.
