Badminton Club

The Badminton Club of IISER Kolkata is a vibrant community of shuttle enthusiasts with two courts (LHC and SAC), rackets, and shuttlecocks available for regular play. The club caters to players of all skill levels, from casual rally sessions to intense competitive matches.

With regular practice sessions, inter-batch tournaments, and selection trials for IISM, the club nurtures talent and sportsmanship while building a strong campus sporting culture.

Events & Activities

Key events include the Fresher's Tournament, Beginner's Tournament, Pro's Tournament, IISM Selection Match and Coaching, Interbatch Tournament, Auction Tournament, and a Farewell Match honoring graduating members. These events promote participation, skill development, and competitive badminton culture across campus.

Achievements

The club regularly participates in the Inter IISER Sports Meet (IISM) and other inter-collegiate badminton tournaments, representing IISER Kolkata with competitive performances.
