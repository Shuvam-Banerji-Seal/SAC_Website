Singularity — The Astronomy Club

Singularity is the official Astronomy club of IISER Kolkata, founded in 2023 by a group of passionate astrophiles. Since then, it has consistently been one of the most active clubs in the institute, with equally active and passionate members. The club aims to foster passion and genuine interest in astronomy through stargazing sessions, student talks, scientist interviews, workshops, projects, quizzes, and discussions.

We are one of the founding clubs of ISAAC — the Indian Synergy of Astronomy and Astrophysics Clubs, a student-led nationwide network formed in June 2025 to unite astronomy and astrophysics clubs across India. ISAAC brings these clubs together to collaborate, learn from one another and share resources, helping the country’s young astronomers grow academically. We are proud that the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India featured and appreciated ISAAC in the May 2026 episode of Mann Ki Baat:

“I would also like to mention ISAAC. It is a student-led nationwide network that connects astronomy and astrophysics clubs.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Mann Ki Baat, 31 May 2026

Events & Activities

The club conducts a lot of events and activities throughout the year, and these are just a few examples.

Student, Faculty, Alumni, and Guest Talks

Singularity hosts a series of talks delivered by students, faculty, alumni, and invited guests. Student speakers get to share an area of interest they are currently working in, or a personal research project of theirs, while faculty, alumni, and guest speakers bring perspectives from active research and careers beyond the institute. The talks are fun and engaging, and give everyone a chance to learn something fresh and interesting.

Workshops

Singularity hosts a variety of workshops, such as water rocketry and astrophotography. These workshops range from fun activities to topics that are useful in actual careers in astronomy, and are conducted by students and faculty alike.

Stargazing

A highlight of Singularity is its stargazing sessions, where students get to observe the night sky and also learn telescope handling techniques. The club conducts these sessions spontaneously, depending on the weather conditions, and they can go on for hours given the chance.

Interviews

The club often gets the chance to interview and interact with many prominent personalities in the field of astrophysics and physics overall, and many insights are gained from them. Some recent interviews were with Prof. Scott Hughes (MIT), Prof. Dipankar Banerjee (IIST), Prof. H.C. Verma, and many more.

Radio Astronomy

Singularity fosters the growth of student-led projects, and one such major activity is radio astronomy, which seeks to observe the cosmos through a different kind of lens. There is a team that builds radio antennas and uses them for scheduled observations, and this team has even been invited to GMRT to hone its skills in this field further.

Discussion Sessions and Quizzes

This is a fairly recent activity started by members of the club, where a topic of interest is chosen and, as the name suggests, discussions on the topic take place. These sessions lean towards the more casual side of the events that Singularity conducts, as the audience gets to participate actively in the discussions and learn more while they’re at it. A fun yearly quiz, titled “The Celestial Quest”, is also held for the students to show off their astronomy knowledge.
